# Parley 1.6.0

## New

- **Azure Translator engine**: a second online option next to DeepL, with **2 million characters a month free** on Microsoft's free tier. Settings → Translation engine → Azure, then paste your key and region ([how to get one](https://learn.microsoft.com/azure/ai-services/translator/create-translator-resource)). If Azure is down or out of characters, Parley falls back to offline.
- **Quick replies**: the speech-bubble button next to the mic opens ten common phrases (*Invite me, please*, *On my way!*, *Thanks for the group!*, *Sorry, I don't speak your language well*…), already translated into Portuguese, Spanish, Russian, German, French, Italian, Korean, Chinese and Japanese. One click and it's copied, ready to paste.
- **Alerts**: messages that mention your character, or words you choose (*Deadmines*, *WTB*, *healer*…), get an orange bar and a soft sound. Settings → *Alert me when a message mentions*.
- **Auto-hide**: tray icon → *Auto-hide overlay*. The overlay fades away when chat is quiet and fades back in as soon as someone writes in another language (or with Ctrl+Shift+T).
- **Tooltips**: hover over any menu option, setting or overlay button to see what it does.
- **Update notice**: Parley checks for new versions about once a day and offers the download in the tray menu.

## Download

- **`Parley-Setup.exe`**: the app plus the WoW addon. Installing over 1.5.0 keeps your settings.
- **`Parley-addon-1.5.0.zip`**: the addon on its own (1.5.0 adds character-name alerts).
