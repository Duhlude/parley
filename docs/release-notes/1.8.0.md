# Parley 1.8.0

## New

- **Never-translate list**: Settings → *Never translate these names or words*. Add your guild name, nicknames or inside jokes and Parley leaves them exactly as written, in chat and in your replies.
- **Burning Crusade and Pandaria place names**: Shattrath, Karazhan, Hellfire Ramparts, Temple of the Jade Serpent, Siege of Orgrimmar and about 55 more are recognised in every WoW language and shown by the name your client uses.

## Updating

On Parley 1.7.0: right-click the tray icon and choose **Update to Parley 1.8.0**. That's it.
On older versions: download **`Parley-Setup.exe`** below and run it; your settings are kept.

The WoW addon is unchanged (still 1.6.0).

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Duhlude/parley/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
