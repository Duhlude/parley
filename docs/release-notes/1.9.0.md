# Parley 1.9.0

## New

- **See what your reply says**: under each reply Parley shows it translated back into your language, so you can check the meaning before you paste it. (Tray icon → *Check replies by translating them back* to turn it off.)
- **Conversation view**: right-click a message → *Conversation with …* to see just that player's messages and your replies, handy for whisper chats while Trade scrolls by.

## Fixed and faster

- Menus no longer show a spinning or resize mouse pointer.
- Arrows in the WoW skins ("You » PT-BR") show up again.
- Clicking a button to close its menu no longer reopens it.
- A stuck offline translator can no longer freeze Parley or block updates.
- Settings are saved safely, with a backup.
- Your never-translate list also covers replies.
- Less CPU: no needless redraws, and much less work while WoW is closed or minimized.
- Plus a dozen smaller fixes: see the changelog.

## Updating

Right-click the tray icon and choose **Update to Parley 1.9.0**. On versions before 1.7.0, download **`Parley-Setup.exe`** below; your settings are kept.

The WoW addon is unchanged (still 1.6.0).

Full list of changes: [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/Duhlude/parley/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md)
