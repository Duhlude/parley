# Parley 1.9.2

## Changed

- **Addon 1.6.1** is marked as up to date for **Retail 12.1.5**, so WoW no longer lists it as out of date there. Nothing else changed in the addon.
- New screenshots and clearer install notes in the docs.

## Updating

Right-click the tray icon and choose **Update to Parley 1.9.2**, or download **`Parley-Setup.exe`** below. The app puts the new addon into your WoW folders automatically; type `/reload` if WoW is running.

- **`Parley-addon-1.6.1.zip`**: the addon on its own.
