# Parley 1.5.0 · first public release

Live chat translation for **World of Warcraft Classic Era** (Retail: beta). Players from Brazil, Russia, Spain, Germany, Korea and everywhere else share your realm; Parley shows what they're saying in your language and translates your replies back into theirs.

## Download

- **`Parley-Setup.exe`**: installs the Windows app and puts the addon into your WoW folder. This is all most people need.
- **`Parley-addon-1.4.0.zip`**: the addon on its own, if you install addons by hand (it's also on CurseForge).

Requires Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and WoW in **Windowed (Fullscreen)** mode. Step-by-step instructions: [INSTALL.txt](https://github.com/Duhlude/parley/blob/main/INSTALL.txt).

## Highlights

- **No account needed.** Translation runs offline on your PC with the open models Firefox uses. Each language downloads once (about 50 MB). Chat never leaves your computer.
- **Optional DeepL.** Paste a DeepL API key for the best quality; Parley falls back to offline if DeepL is down or out of characters.
- **Reply in their language.** Click a message, type in English, press Enter: Parley translates it, adds `/w Name` (or `/p`, `/4` …) and copies it for you to paste.
- **Knows gamer talk.** Dungeon and city names in every client language (Мертвые копи, Ventobravo, 死亡矿井 → Deadmines, Stormwind), slang like *vlw, спс, лс, хил*, and "I can tank" → *posso tankar*.
- **Five skins**: Classic WoW, Retail WoW, Dragonflight, WoW chat frame and a modern dark theme, with matching menus and settings.
- **In your language.** The app, installer and addon follow your Windows / WoW language (German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Chinese).
- **Extras**: voice replies (Ctrl+Shift+Y), right-click to copy / reply / mute, click-through overlay, daily chat history.

## Retail (beta)

The addon loads on Retail 12.1 but hasn't had much testing there. During Mythic+ keys, PvP matches and boss fights Blizzard hides chat from all addons, so Parley pauses and says so in the overlay. Please [open an issue](https://github.com/Duhlude/parley/issues) if anything looks wrong.

## Good to know

- Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC" because the installer isn't code-signed yet. Click **More info → Run anyway**.
- Parley never reads game memory, injects code or presses keys; it reads a small strip of pixels the addon draws. It's a fan project, not endorsed by Blizzard. Use at your own risk.
